# Maven Silicon Revision

Double-click **Open-Maven-Silicon-Revision.cmd** to use the local app in Chrome. Your current focus is **Digital · Assignment 7**.

## Keep the outside simple

```text
README.md
Updates.md
To-Ask.md
Open-Maven-Silicon-Revision.cmd
_internal/
```

`Updates.md` and `To-Ask.md` are private working files maintained from saved app data. The local companion refreshes them every 10 seconds while the app is running. Manual edits are preserved under `_internal/sources/file-inbox/` before a generated file is refreshed. Add new questions and responses through the app.

All application code, course material, original PDFs, tools, guides, tests, backups and supporting files stay inside `_internal/`. Hidden Git metadata remains at the repository root.

The existing three-page **Digital Review 03** PDF is maintained in the local app, together with page previews. Its PDF bytes are preserved. This is the existing local copy; its matching sent email has not been retrieved through the connected account.

## What “up to date” means

The app identifies its **local workspace** and refreshes from its saved database. Failed refreshes disable further writes until recovery, and older responses cannot replace newer data. The local app and the earlier hosted app have separate databases; a local update does not mean the hosted site has been redeployed.

The application-code repository is public. Working Markdown, course questions, handwriting, original reviews, runtime data and correspondence stay private and are excluded from Git.
